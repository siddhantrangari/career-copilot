STEP 1 — Research Prompt (paste into Claude before contacting anyone)
I want to reach out to [Engineering Manager / CTO] at [COMPANY]. Research them thoroughly — website, blog, recent news, job postings, Glassdoor. Identify 3–5 pressing technical challenges they face RIGHT NOW in engineering, AI, or product delivery. For each: (1) The challenge — specific not generic. (2) Evidence with source + date. (3) One sharp insight I can use to start a conversation. Then give me a one-line curiosity hook for a LinkedIn DM — something referencing a real problem that makes them want to reply. NOT a job application tone. A peer who noticed something interesting.
STEP 2A — Connection Request (300 chars max — send FIRST)
Hi [Name] — I came across [Company]'s work on [specific thing from research]. I have an interesting perspective on [the problem area]. Would love to connect.
STEP 2B — First DM after they accept (within 24hrs)
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I was going through [specific article/blog/job posting] and noticed [Company] seems to be focused on [specific challenge]. I've spent the last few years working on exactly this — at Philips I built real-time Kafka pipelines for clinical data at sub-second latency, and recently I architected a multi-agent AI platform with LLM failover and 12+ external integrations. Is [specific challenge] something your team is actively working through? Would love to hear your perspective — happy to jump on a 15-minute call if useful.
STEP 2C — Peer / Referral DM (Indian engineers at target company)
Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching [Company]'s engineering team — [genuine observation about their work]. I'm a senior full-stack + AI engineer from Mumbai (ex-Philips Innovation Campus, 12 years) exploring global roles. [Company] is high on my list. Would love to hear about your experience there — what the engineering culture is like, what kind of problems the team is solving. Open to a 15-minute chat? No agenda, genuinely just want to learn from someone on the inside.
STEP 2D — Follow-up Day 4 (if no reply — ONE follow-up only)
Hi [Name] — following up on my message from [day]. Came across [another specific article/news about Company] since then — [one new observation that adds value]. Still curious about your take on [the challenge]. Happy to keep it to 10 minutes — whatever works for you.
STEP 3 — The 15-Min Call Script
OPENING: "Thanks for the time. I'll keep this to 15 min. I've been researching [Company] and had hypotheses about [area] — wanted to validate them with someone on the inside." QUESTIONS (pick 2–3): → "It looks like [challenge from research] — is that something your team is actively working on?" → "What's the biggest technical problem you're solving in the next 6 months?" → "What engineer profile is hardest to hire for right now?" → "If you were building your ideal candidate, what must they have?" → "Are there any internal roles not posted yet that might be relevant?" CLOSING: "This was really insightful. Based on what you shared about [problem], I'd love to put together a quick 2-page doc with thoughts on how I'd approach it. Can I send that across?" NOTES AFTER CALL — write immediately: • 2–3 real problems they confirmed (in their exact words) • Whether a role exists or is upcoming • Who else to talk to
STEP 4 — WIN Document Builder Prompt (paste into Claude after your call)
I spoke to [Name], [Title] at [Company]. They confirmed: PROBLEM 1: [their exact words] PROBLEM 2: [their exact words] PROBLEM 3: [their exact words] My background: - Philips: real-time Kafka→AWS S3 patient monitoring (70% load reduction, sub-second latency, 30-40% MTTR improvement) - MyAgentFirm: multi-tenant AI agent platform — 24 API routes, 6 async workers, LLM failover (DeepInfra→Groq→Together AI), 12+ integrations - Growlogic/Eeko.ai: Azure AKS microservices (6 Spring Boot services, WhatsApp API, ArgoCD GitOps) - Stack: Java Spring Boot, Node.js/Fastify, React, Python, Docker, Kubernetes, Kafka, PostgreSQL, LangChain, RAG, MCP Build a 2-page WIN document: 1. THEIR 3 PROBLEMS — clearly stated with evidence 2. MY SOLUTIONS — consultant-grade, specific and actionable per problem 3. WHY ME — which of my real projects proves I can solve each (CAR format: Challenge→Action→Result) 4. 30-DAY PLAN — what I do in my first month 5. CLOSING — one sentence inviting a follow-up call Tone: consultant presenting a proposal, not a job seeker. Format for slide deck export.
STEP 4B — Send WIN Doc Message
Hi [Name], really enjoyed our conversation on [day]. Based on what you shared about [specific problem], I put together a short 2-page doc with thoughts on how I'd approach it — and how my experience maps directly to what you're solving. Happy to walk you through it on a quick call, or feel free to share it with whoever's relevant. [Attach WIN doc as PDF]
