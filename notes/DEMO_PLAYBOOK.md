# Career Copilot — Interactive Demo Playbook

## 1. Targeted Job Roles Vault & Granola AI Engineer
> Goal: Demonstrate how Career Copilot saves and analyzes dream job roles with match scoring and 1-click execution.

### Steps
1. Navigate to **`Pipeline / Tracker`** in the top navigation.
2. Ensure **`Targeted Roles Vault`** tab is active.
3. Observe the pre-seeded **Granola AI Engineer** role card:
   - Match Fit Score (~94%)
   - Strategic Conviction callout
   - Required tech stack & superpowers to showcase
4. Test 1-Click Action Buttons:
   - Click **`🎯 Tailor ATS`**: Opens the ATS studio with Granola job description pre-populated.
   - Click **`🏆 WIN Deck`**: Opens the 30-60-90 day executive pitcher pre-filled for Granola.
   - Click **`🤝 Find Leads`**: Navigates to company researcher pre-loaded for Granola.
   - Click **`📋 Track Kanban`**: Adds Granola opportunity to the Kanban pipeline.
   - Click **`✨ Ask Nova`**: Opens the Nova Copilot drawer with an executive attack plan prompt for Granola.

## 2. Nova AI Copilot & NxtJob 58 Success Stories
> Goal: Demonstrate asking Nova about any of the 58 video case studies and executing autonomous career tools.

### Steps
1. Click the floating **`✨ Nova Copilot`** button in the bottom right or go to **`Nova AI`** in navbar.
2. Ask questions such as:
   - *"How did Anurag get 30+ interviews and a 50% hike?"*
   - *"Generate a 30-60-90 Day WIN Document for Stripe Staff Engineer."*
   - *"Tailor my master resume for Razorpay Director of Tech."*
3. Observe Nova output interactive action cards with 1-click copy, slide navigation, and Markdown downloads.
